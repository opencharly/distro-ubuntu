# 2026.296.0855 — chore(clawsweeper): pin the review candy to the option-forwarding commit

## Summary

Moves the review image's candy pin to `layer-clawsweeper#7` (`a9f48c9`), whose entrypoint forwards extra
engine options to the engine instead of fixing the whole invocation.

That is what lets the advisory workflow use capabilities the image already ships:

- **`--body-file`** — the engine reviews that exact body and assesses its proof claims against it;
- **`--output-retention debug`** — round-over-round history, so a later review verifies fixes instead of
  re-raising resolved findings;
- `--additional-policy`, `--verbose`, and anything else the engine grows.

The image still decides the mode (`--local-range`) and the output format.
