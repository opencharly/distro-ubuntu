# 2026.296.2114 — feat(ci): Blacksmith parity fleet shape — 9x4/16, 4x8/32, 1x16/64

## Summary

Replace the KubeVirt CI fleet's tier templates with the operator-approved
**Blacksmith parity shape**: 4 GB of guest RAM per vCPU on every tier, so a
Blacksmith label no longer under-delivers RAM.

| VMs | Guest | Runners/VM | Slots | Blacksmith labels |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 9 | 4 vCPU / 16 GB | 1 | 9 | `blacksmith-2vcpu-ubuntu-2404` **and** `blacksmith-4vcpu-ubuntu-2404` |
| 4 | 8 vCPU / 32 GB | 2 | 8 | `blacksmith-8vcpu-ubuntu-2404` |
| 1 | 16 vCPU / 64 GB | 2 | 2 | `blacksmith-16vcpu-ubuntu-2404` **and** `blacksmith-32vcpu-ubuntu-2404` |

- **`githubrunner-ubuntu-vmi-4vcpu`** is new (4 cores / 16 GB). It carries both
  the 2-vCPU and 4-vCPU tags: an **upgrade** claim (4 ≥ 2), stated in the
  template comment.
- **`-8vcpu`** moves 20 GB → 32 GB; **`-16vcpu`** moves 32 GB → 64 GB.
- **The 16-vCPU tier also carries `blacksmith-32vcpu-ubuntu-2404`**, as directed.
  The template comment records verbatim that this is an **UNDER-provision**
  (Blacksmith's 32-vCPU type is 32 vCPU / 128 GB, so a job routed here gets half
  the cores and half the RAM), that the label is an operator routing decision
  rather than a measurement, and that sizing the VM to 32/128 does not fit: the
  approved shape is **336 GB** (9×16 + 4×32 + 1×64), and resizing this VM from
  64 to 128 adds 64 GiB, bringing the fleet to **400 GiB — past the 392 GiB
  cluster**.
- **Retired (R5):** `githubrunner-ubuntu-vmi-2vcpu` and `-vmi-32vcpu` are deleted
  in the same cutover, and the stale pool comments (`27 (9x2, 6x8, 2x16, 1x32)`)
  are replaced with the new shape.

`cpu.model: host-passthrough` stays on every tier (the measured route to a guest
`/dev/kvm`). `devices.host_devices` is deliberately not added: KubeVirt rejects
it at admission on this cluster (`spec.HostDevices: Host Devices feature gate is
not enabled in kubevirt-config`) and the host-device address pools are
PCI/MDEV/USB only, so it cannot carry a character device (`opencharly/spec#278`).

The base `githubrunner-ubuntu-vmi` template stays: it is the venue of the
`check-githubrunner-ubuntu-vmi` bed, not a fleet tier, and carries no blacksmith
label.
