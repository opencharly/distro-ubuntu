### Why this is not cosmetic

These refs are **not broken today** — they name *old* charly tags that still contain the
directories. That is precisely the failure this cutover exists to remove: **content frozen at a
stale pin, with every gate staying green.** charly's `main` no longer has those directories at all
(`opencharly/charly#451`), so the next pin bump would turn a silent staleness into a hard break.
